OpenAI just made its cheapest model the default for free users. Quiet change, big effect.

Most people will never notice. But if you run a small business on the free tier, your answers just got faster and, from what I have seen, a bit shallower on long documents.

What I would do: keep using free for drafting and quick questions. For anything that needs careful reading, paste less and ask more specific questions. That gets you most of the paid quality back.

The part I like: they were upfront that it is a cost decision. Companies rarely say that.

A clinic in Lahore started using an AI tool to write the first draft of patient summaries. Doctors still sign every one.

The reported result: about an hour saved per doctor per day. Not because the AI is brilliant, but because the boring part of the job was the typing.

That pattern keeps showing up. The wins are not in replacing the expert. They are in removing the paperwork around the expert.

If you are looking for where AI fits in your own work, start with the part you already do on autopilot.
